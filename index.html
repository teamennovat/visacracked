Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is Visa Cracked?

We aren’t a paperwork agency; we are an interview coaching platform. While most agencies focus on filling out forms, we focus on the person behind the desk. We provide hyper-realistic mock interviews and expert coaching to help you pass the high-pressure consular interview.

I’ve already been rejected once. Can you still help me?

Absolutely. In fact, many of our clients come to us after a "214(b)" or similar rejection. We help you analyze your previous interview, identify "red flags" in your answers, and build a stronger strategy for your next attempt.

What if I don't see my specific visa type listed?

While we list the most common visas (Student, Work, Tourist), our experts are equipped to handle niche categories. Simply contact us, and we can tailor a simulation for your specific situation.

Which countries do you cover?

We specialize in high-scrutiny destinations including the USA, UK, Canada, Schengen Area (Germany, France, Spain, etc.), Japan, and Australia.

Do you guarantee that my visa will be approved?

No one can legally guarantee a visa approval as that decision rests solely with the consular officer. However, our 98% success rate reflects how much of an impact proper preparation has on the final outcome.

Is the coaching done in person or online?

All our sessions are conducted online via video call. This allows us to work with clients globally and mimics the "window-to-window" feel of a real consulate interview.
