Family / Dependent Visa

This visa lets family members join their relatives living abroad. Provide proof of relationship, accommodation, and financial support for a smooth process.

Overview

A family or dependent visa allows relatives to join a family member living abroad. It enables spouses, children, or other dependents to live legally in the host country.

Required Documents

Typically needed:

  • Valid passports for all applicants

  • Recent biometric photos

  • Proof of relationship (marriage certificate, birth certificate)

  • Sponsor’s residence permit or visa copy

  • Proof of accommodation

  • Proof of financial support

  • Completed visa application forms

  • Payment receipt for the visa fee

Application Requirements

  • Applicants must be legally recognized dependents of the sponsor.

  • Proof of financial means and housing is usually required.

  • No criminal record or security concerns.

Things to Consider During Application

  • Gather all relationship documents carefully.

  • Ensure sponsor meets income and accommodation requirements.

  • Apply well in advance, as family visas may take longer.

Processing Time

Typically 4–12 weeks, depending on the host country and number of applicants.

Final Tips

Provide clear proof of relationship and financial stability. Proper preparation helps ensure smooth processing for the entire family.
